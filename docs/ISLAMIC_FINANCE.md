# Islamic finance first

Faisla compares **Shariah-compliant financing by default**. Conventional (interest-based) financing appears only when
the owner picks "Islamic and conventional" on the Financing page or in Settings. Even then it is labelled and shown
for comparison only: the recommendation is always a Shariah-compliant row (cash, the supplier's deferred-price plan,
or Islamic financing).

Research checked on 10 Oct 2026. SBP's own site blocked most automated reads, so some SBP facts come through bank
pages and news reports. These are marked below.

## Why

- **26th Constitutional Amendment (Oct 2024).** Article 38(f) now reads "eliminate riba completely before the first
  day of January, two thousand twenty-eight". The old text said "as early as possible". The committee draft said "as
  far as practicable, by 1 January 2028". We could not read the Gazette, so the exact enacted wording is not fully
  verified. "Before 1 January 2028" is safe to say.
  - [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/pakistan/pakistan-sets-jan-1-2028-deadline-to-eliminate-riba-or-interest-from-country-2576082)
  - [National Assembly summary](https://na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/67a496ea68bb9_790.pdf)
  - [Draft wording](https://english.aaj.tv/news/amp/330384744)
- **Federal Shariat Court, 28 April 2022.** The court set 31 Dec 2027 for the complete elimination of riba. SBP and
  NBP withdrew their appeals in Nov 2022.

## Products (how Pakistani Islamic banks finance machinery)

| Mode | How it works | Pricing | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Diminishing Musharakah** | Bank and customer co-own the asset. The customer pays rent on the bank's share and buys the bank's units over time until it owns everything. | KIBOR + agreed spread, repriced periodically | [Meezan](https://www.meezanbank.com/commercial-banking/) lists Diminishing Musharakah and Ijarah for "plant and machinery"; [Bank Al Habib Islamic](https://islamic.bankalhabib.com/sme-banking); [SBP Shariah Standard on Diminishing Musharakah, IBD Circular 02/2013](https://www.sbp.org.pk/circulars/ibd-circular-no-02-of-2013); [DIB Pakistan: "Relevant KIBOR + Agreed Basis Points"](https://www.dibpak.com/?p=340) |
| **Ijarah** | Bank owns the asset and leases it. The customer pays a security deposit and monthly rentals; the bank as owner is responsible for takaful. At the end, ownership passes by a separate sale (often at a token amount, or against the deposit) or a gift. | Rentals benchmarked to KIBOR + spread | [Meezan Ijarah](https://www.meezanbank.com/?p=10); [Bank Alfalah FAQ (takaful by the owner)](https://www.bankalfalah.com/islamic-premier/consumer-banking/alfalah-islamic-auto-finance/faqs); AAOIFI Shariah Standard 9, adopted by [SBP in 2010](https://www.sbp.org.pk/ibd/2010/C1.htm) |
| **Murabaha** | Bank buys the asset and sells it to the customer at cost plus profit. The deferred price is fixed at the contract and paid in installments. Meezan uses it mainly for raw materials and working capital, so Faisla offers it as a preset rather than in the demo set. | Fixed deferred price | [Meezan](https://www.meezanbank.com/commercial-banking/); AAOIFI Shariah Standard 8 ([SBP 2010](https://www.sbp.org.pk/ibd/2010/C1.htm), [compendium IFPD 07/2024](https://www.sbp.org.pk/ifpd/2024/C7.htm)) |
| **SBP Islamic LTFF (ILTFF)** | Islamic counterpart of LTFF for exporters buying new machinery, offered through Islamic banks. SBP funds the banks on a Mudaraba basis. Up to 10 years, including up to 2 years' grace. | End-user rate "3% less than the SBP policy rate" (same as LTFF) | [ABL export finance](https://www.abl.com/business-banking/trade-services/export-finances/); [SBP incentive page](https://www.sbp.org.pk/incen/Export-3.asp) (search snippet only); [link to the policy rate, July 2022](https://www.sbp.org.pk/circulars/ihsmefd-circular-no-11-of-2022); [spread cut to 3%, Dec 2022](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40217364) |

Common features, which Faisla shows as **Shariah notes** in the Assumptions card:

- Asset-backed.
- No compounding.
- Late payment goes to charity under the customer's undertaking. It is "not part of the bank's income"
  ([Bank of Khyber](https://www.bok.com.pk/sites/default/files/2026-04/Islamic%20Banking%20Products%20and%20their%20difference%20with%20Conventional%20Banking%20....docx);
  AAOIFI Shariah Standard 3, adopted by [SBP](https://www.sbp.org.pk/ibd/2010/C1.htm)).
- Takaful instead of conventional insurance.
- The bank's Shariah board approves the product.

  ([ARY](https://arynews.tv/sbp-retains-policy-rate-at-11-5-percent)).

Meezan's home Diminishing Musharakah reverts to
  "KIBOR + 3%". We found no published SME spread.

- On 27 Jul 2026 the ECC approved a fixed-rate Long-Term Export Growth Financing Facility: 2% for two years, then 5%.
  We found no Islamic version and no effective date yet.

## The supplier's "0% markup" plan is a deferred-price sale, not interest

A credit sale at a higher deferred price (bay' muajjal / bay' bi-thaman ajil) is permissible when the price is fixed
at the contract and nothing is added for late payment:

- **IIFA Resolution 51 (2/6), 1990:** "It is permissible to fix an increased price for a commodity sold on deferred
  payment, as compared to its cash price." It also says no amount may be added for delay
  ([IIFA](https://iifa-aifi.org/en/32398.html)).
- **Mufti Taqi Usmani, *An Introduction to Islamic Finance*:** once fixed, the price "can never be increased" (via
  [islamqa.org](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa/7738/murabaha-house-financing-when-deferred-payments-are-not-fixed/)).

So Faisla does not call the plan "hidden interest". It labels it `A, deferred-price plan ("0%")` and reports the
**effective yearly cost of paying later** against the cash price: "Paying later costs 16.9% a year". It is still the
dearest way to pay in the demo.

## How each mode is modelled (`src/lib/finance.ts`)

All modes share the same timeline:

- **Month 0** is delivery. The customer's contribution (or security deposit) is paid then.
- **Months 1…n:** equal monthly payments, i = rate ÷ 12 on the outstanding amount.
- **Tax** savings arrive at the end of each tax year (29%).
- **Discounting:** 15% before tax, 10.65% after tax.

| Mode | Cash flows | Tax (customer) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diminishing Musharakah / Islamic LTFF | 10% own share at delivery. 36 installments of the annuity on the bank's 90% share. Each installment = rent on the bank's remaining share + purchase of its units. | Rent (profit) deductible as it accrues. The customer depreciates the full cost: 25% initial allowance, then 15% reducing balance. |
| Murabaha | 10% advance. 36 equal installments of a deferred price fixed at the contract (same annuity maths). Not shifted by the KIBOR stress test. | Profit part deductible; full-cost depreciation as owner. |
| Ijarah | 10% security deposit at delivery. 36 rentals = annuity on the bank's cost net of the deposit. At month 36 the deposit is returned and paid back as the purchase price, so the net flow is nil. | Every rental is deductible as paid (ITO 2001 s.28(1)(b), lease rentals to a scheduled bank: [PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/pakistan/corporate/deductions)). No depreciation during the lease. After transfer: 15% reducing balance on the purchase price from the next tax year, with no initial allowance (used plant is excluded under s.23: [pkrevenue](https://pkrevenue.com/initial-allowance-deduction-under-updated-ito-2001/)). Remainder written off in year 10. |
| Conventional loan / LTFF ("both" only) | Same flows as Diminishing Musharakah. | Interest deductible; full-cost depreciation. |

**Tax note.** We found no FBR text stating that Murabaha or Diminishing Musharakah profit is "profit on debt" under
s.28(1)(a). Treating it as deductible is standard practice, and Faisla lists it as an assumption.
